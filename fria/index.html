Is a FRIA required for your AI system?
Article 27 requires deployers of certain high-risk AI systems to conduct a fundamental rights impact assessment before use.

Public body / body governed by public law: Any entity established to serve a general interest, mainly financed by public authorities, or subject to public management supervision. Examples: municipalities, public universities, state hospitals, publicly-owned enterprises.

Private entity providing public services: A private company that delivers services typically associated with the public sector — such as private healthcare providers, private schools, private housing authorities, or outsourced social services. If your AI system affects access to these services, a FRIA is likely required.
