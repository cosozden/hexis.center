**1. AI System Inventory Template** (.xlsx)
Register every AI system in your organisation. 7 validated columns with dropdown menus for risk level, data type, and organisational role. Designed for multi-system tracking with built-in data validation.

**2. Risk Classification Worksheet** (.pdf)
A 7-step decision tree that follows the exact EU AI Act classification logic: Art. 5 (prohibited) → Art. 6(1) (Annex I) → Art. 6(2) (Annex III) → Art. 6(3) (exception check) → Art. 50 (transparency) → Art. 51–56 (GPAI) → Minimal risk. **3. EU AI Act Compliance Assessment** (.xlsx)
25 obligations mapped with exact article references, enforcement deadlines, and required evidence. Gap assessment worksheet for scoring each system's compliance status. Dashboard with priority matrix and compliance score.

**4. Compliance Roadmap Template** (.xlsx)
Phased action plan with priorities, effort estimates, responsible person assignment, and a 12-month timeline view. Dashboard tracks completion by phase and generates milestone reports. Designed for presenting progress to management.

**5. Quick Start Guide** (.pdf)
Step-by-step instructions for using the entire kit. Explains the ORIENT methodology (Observe → Risk → Identify → Evaluate → Navigate → Track) that structures your compliance journey. Complete the full kit in 4–6 hours. No prior EU AI Act knowledge required.
