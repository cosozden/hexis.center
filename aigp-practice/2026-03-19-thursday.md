### Q1 — Domain III.A (AI System Design Governance) | Bloom: Apply

A mid-size financial services company is developing an AI-powered credit scoring model. During the design phase, the project team identifies that the training dataset contains historical lending decisions that may reflect past discriminatory practices. According to governance best practices, what should the team do FIRST?

A) Proceed with development using the existing dataset and add bias testing during the validation phase
B) Conduct a data impact assessment to identify and document potential sources of bias before model training begins
C) Replace the entire dataset with synthetic data to eliminate any risk of historical bias
D) Escalate the issue to the legal department and pause all development until regulatory guidance is received

---

**Answer: B**

**Açıklama:** Governance best practice, model eğitimine başlamadan ÖNCE veri kalitesini ve potansiyel bias kaynaklarını değerlendirmeyi gerektirir. Data impact assessment, hangi değişkenlerin proxy discrimination yaratabilceğini belgeleyerek tasarım aşamasında müdahale imkanı verir. A seçeneği riski geç aşamaya erteler — maliyetli ve riskli. C seçeneği aşırı tepki — sentetik veri kendi sorunlarını getirir. D seçeneği gereksiz yere tüm geliştirmeyi durdurur; governance ekibi bu değerlendirmeyi yapabilir.

**BoK Referansı:** III.A — AI system design governance; data quality and bias assessment
**İlgili Standart:** ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Clause 6.1.2 (AI risk assessment), EU AI Act Article 10 (Data and data governance)

### Q2 — Domain III.B (Data Governance for AI) | Bloom: Analyze

An organization uses a third-party vendor's pre-trained language model and fine-tunes it on proprietary customer interaction data. During a governance review, the team discovers that the vendor's original training data composition is not fully documented. Which risk does this scenario PRIMARILY illustrate?

A) Model drift risk due to distribution shift between pre-training and fine-tuning data
B) Supply chain transparency risk due to insufficient documentation of upstream data provenance
C) Intellectual property risk due to potential copyright violations in the vendor's training data
D) Operational risk due to the organization's dependency on a single vendor for model infrastructure

---

**Answer: B**

**Açıklama:** Bu senaryo doğrudan supply chain transparency sorununu gösteriyor. Üçüncü parti modelin eğitim verisinin bileşimi belgelenmemişse, organizasyon kendi fine-tuning verisinin üzerine hangi bias'ları veya kalite sorunlarını miras aldığını bilemez. NIST AI RMF'de "data provenance" ve EU AI Act Article 10'da veri yönetişimi gereksinimleri bu riski kapsar. C seçeneği geçerli bir endişe ama soru "primarily" diyor — governance açısından asıl mesele belgeleme eksikliği. A ve D yan risklerdir.

**BoK Referansı:** III.B — Data governance, training and testing data management
**İlgili Standart:** NIST AI RMF MAP 3.4 (data provenance), EU AI Act Article 10(2)(f) (data governance measures)

### Q3 — Domain III.C (Release, Monitoring & Maintenance) | Bloom: Apply

A healthcare AI system that assists radiologists in detecting lung nodules has been in production for 14 months. Recent internal audits show that the model's sensitivity has decreased by 8% compared to its initial deployment metrics. The AI governance committee must decide the appropriate response. What is the MOST appropriate governance action?

A) Immediately decommission the system and revert to fully manual review until a new model is trained
B) Initiate the organization's model performance degradation protocol, including root cause analysis and stakeholder notification, while maintaining human oversight safeguards
C) Retrain the model on the most recent 6 months of data and redeploy without additional validation since the original model was already approved
D) Increase the human review rate from 10% to 25% of AI-flagged cases as a temporary compensating control while continuing to use the current model indefinitely

---

**Answer: B**

**Açıklama:** Post-market monitoring'de performans düşüşü tespit edildiğinde, governance yaklaşımı önceden tanımlanmış degradation protokolünü başlatmaktır: root cause analysis, etki değerlendirmesi, paydaş bildirimi ve düzeltici eylem planı. A seçeneği orantısız — hastalar da zarar görür. C seçeneği validation'ı atlıyor, bu yüksek riskli sağlık sisteminde kabul edilemez. D seçeneği geçici kontrol olarak faydalı ama "indefinitely" ifadesi onu yetersiz kılıyor — kalıcı çözüm planı gerekir.

**BoK Referansı:** III.C — Release management, post-deployment monitoring, model maintenance
**İlgili Standart:** EU AI Act Article 72 (Post-market monitoring), ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Clause 9.1 (Monitoring, measurement, analysis and evaluation)
