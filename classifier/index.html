How It Works

7-Step Classification Wizard

1Prohibited Practices

Article 5 Check

Does your system fall under prohibited AI practices? Social scoring, real-time biometric identification, subliminal manipulation — if yes, classification stops here.

2Product Safety

Annex I — Article 6(1)

Is your AI a safety component of a product covered by EU harmonisation legislation? Machinery, medical devices, vehicles, toys, lifts, and more.

3High-Risk Domains

Annex III — Article 6(2)

8 high-risk areas: biometrics, critical infrastructure, education, employment, essential services, law enforcement, migration, justice. Includes Article 6(3) exception check.

4Transparency

Article 50 Obligations

Chatbots, deepfakes, emotion recognition, biometric categorisation — specific disclosure requirements even for non-high-risk systems.

5GPAI

Articles 51–56

General-purpose AI model obligations: provider vs. deployer, systemic risk thresholds (10^25 FLOP), and downstream integration duties.

6–7Assessment

FRIA + Result Card

Fundamental rights impact screening and your complete result: risk level, applicable articles, obligation table, enforcement deadlines, and KVKK cross-reference (if applicable).

Output

What you get

  • Risk level classification: Prohibited, High-Risk, Limited Risk (Transparency), GPAI, or Minimal
  • Applicable EU AI Act articles with direct links to the regulation text
  • Obligation summary table: what you must do, by when
  • Enforcement deadline countdown (February 2025 → August 2028)
  • KVKK cross-reference block for Turkey-operating organisations
  • Direct link to the Compliance Checklist filtered by your risk level
