The ORIENT Process

Six steps from awareness to compliance

The platform walks you through a structured governance process. Each step builds on the last. AI assists at every stage.

1

Observe

Register your AI systems. Describe what they do, what data they use, and who is responsible. AI helps extract structured data from free-text descriptions.

2

Risk

Classify each system's risk level under the EU AI Act. The decision-tree wizard follows Articles 5, 6, 50, and 51-56. AI enriches with contextual analysis.

3

Identify

Map your legal obligations based on risk level. Each item includes article references, deadlines, and AI-guided "how to comply" advisory.

4

Evaluate

Assess your governance maturity across oversight, monitoring, and documentation. The Governance Activation Matrix reveals your critical gaps.

5

Navigate

AI generates a prioritised action plan based on your obligations, gaps, and resources. Discuss and refine through conversation.

6

Track

Monitor compliance scores over time, set review schedules, and generate audience-adapted reports for board, DPO, or auditor audiences.
